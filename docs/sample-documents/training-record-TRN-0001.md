## Why this matters

orientation is on file at all, on top of the expired LOTO certification specifically. This is one of four
contractors in the dataset (CTR-0003, CTR-0007, CTR-0011, CTR-0014) with a documented training gap on a
safety-critical topic at the time of an associated incident — two from Redline Welding & Fabrication
(LOTO, Confined Space) and two from Vantage Industrial Coatings (Contractor Safety Orientation, LOTO).

The pattern this is meant to demonstrate: these are not six unrelated contractor errors. They trace back
to a gap in Rex's own contractor authorization process — work was authorized (or at least not stopped)
without a training-status check against records like this one. See control CTL-0004 (Contractor LOTO
Verification Gate) and audit finding AUD-0002.
