# Rex Industrial Manufacturing — Audit Finding

**Audit Finding ID:** AUD-0001
**Facility:** Rex North Plant (FAC-001)
**Audit Date:** December 8, 2025
**Auditor:** Meridian Safety Consulting (external EHS audit firm)
**Finding Category:** Machine Safety
**Severity:** High
**Status:** Closed

## Finding
Point-of-operation guarding on punch press equipment was found defeated during the walkthrough; this
matches a condition noted in a prior internal control assessment and at least one recent incident report.

## Referenced Records
- **Related SOP:** SOP-0004 — Machine Guarding Standard (v2.2, owned by Rebecca Flores, EHS Coordinator)
- **Related Control:** CTL-0001 — Fixed Machine Guard Interlock Program

## Auditor Note
This finding was reached independently of Rex Industrial's internal incident log and control-assessment
history — the auditor walked the floor and observed the defeated guard directly. That it lines up with
Rex's own internal record (control assessment CTLA series showing CTL-0001 oscillating between "Partially
Effective" and "Ineffective," and incident INC-0008 among others) is exactly the kind of independent
corroboration that should elevate this from "one inspector's opinion" to a confirmed systemic gap, not a
one-off observation.

## Disposition
Finding closed following remediation action tracked separately in the corrective action log (see actions
targeting related_control_id = CTL-0001). Auditor recommended the facility verify remediation holds across
all shifts, not just the shift observed during the walkthrough — informed by the fact that this exact
control had already "passed" a review once before (CTLA-0001, Effective, Feb 2025) and regressed.
