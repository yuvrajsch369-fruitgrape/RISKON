# Rex Industrial Manufacturing — Standard Operating Procedure (Excerpt)

**SOP ID:** SOP-0004
**Title:** Machine Guarding Standard
**Category:** Machine Safety
**Version:** 2.2
**Effective Date:** August 16, 2024
**Next Review Date:** August 16, 2027
**Owner:** Rebecca Flores, EHS Coordinator
**Scope:** Company-wide (all Rex Industrial facilities)
**Status:** Active

## 1. Purpose
This procedure establishes minimum requirements for machine guarding at all Rex Industrial Manufacturing
facilities to prevent contact with points of operation, ingoing nip points, rotating parts, and flying
debris.

## 2. Requirements (excerpt)
2.1 All point-of-operation guards must remain in place and functional during normal operation.

2.2 Guards shall not be removed, propped open, or defeated for any purpose, **including jam-clearing**,
without first isolating the machine per SOP-0001 (Lockout/Tagout Procedure).

2.3 Interlocked guards must be verified functional as part of the equipment's preventive maintenance
cycle (see related control CTL-0001, Fixed Machine Guard Interlock Program) and during any inspection
that includes the equipment in scope.

2.4 Any observed guard defeat — whether during normal operation, inspection, or audit — must be reported
as an incident (Unsafe Condition or Near Miss, as applicable) regardless of whether contact or injury
occurred.
